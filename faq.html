About Mappstack

What is Mappstack?

Mappstack is a software services company. You hire it for whole software projects, end to end, new or existing, in any industry and at any size. Every system is mapped first, built from the map and checked against it, and every later change starts on the map.

What does Mappstack do?

Mappstack delivers whole software projects end to end, new or existing, in any industry. It also rescues apps built with AI and takes over as fractional CTO. When someone else builds, Mappstack checks the work. It connects systems and moves them off legacy. It puts AI to work with permissions, audit trails and human approval.

Who is Mappstack for?

Mappstack is for companies that need software built, rescued or changed and want to understand it after it ships. It takes on greenfield and brownfield projects in any industry and at any size.

Where is Mappstack based?

Mappstack is an Australian company. It is the registered business name of SY Innovations Pty Ltd, ABN 38 668 523 760. It works remotely with clients anywhere, with in-person visits in Australia, India and the EU when needed.

Is Stackure part of Mappstack?

How It Works

How does Mappstack work?

Mappstack works in 3 steps: Map, Build and Evolve. Map: a new system is designed on the map before any code is written, and an existing system is mapped from its code. Build: people, AI agents or both build from the map, and every build is checked against it. Evolve: every change starts on the map, so the map and the system never drift apart.

What does Mappstack mean by a map?

A Mappstack map is a model of a software system that people and AI can read. It starts with the whole picture and zooms in only where needed. Each part is defined once, so every view of it updates together, and missing pieces show up in the design, not in production. The maps model software systems. They are not geographic maps.

How does Mappstack use AI, and how is it checked?

Mappstack uses AI agents to build from the map, alone or alongside people, depending on the project. Every build is checked against the map and every change starts on the map, so the system stays explainable however it was written. When Mappstack puts AI to work in a client's business, it does so with permissions, audit trails and human approval.

Who does the work at Mappstack?

At Mappstack, people, AI agents or both do the work, chosen per project. Clients don't need to manage that unless they have a preference.

Which stacks and industries does Mappstack work with?

Mappstack works in any industry and with any stack. Technology is chosen to fit the project, or the client's own stack is used when they require it. Projects start from proven building blocks, such as Stackure for passwordless auth, and from experience on past and present projects.
